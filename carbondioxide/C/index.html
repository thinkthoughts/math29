Earth · Physical context

The carbon cycle precedes its models.

Earth's oceans exchange carbon dioxide with the atmosphere while circulating carbon through living organisms, seawater chemistry, sediments, and geological reservoirs. Ocean carbon dioxide removal (CDR) proposals seek to influence parts of that cycle. Whether an intervention produces durable additional removal—and what ecological consequences it may have—must be determined from physical evidence, appropriate observations, and adequately validated models.

Software can help evaluate possibilities, uncertainties, and monitoring strategies. Measurements of atmospheric CO₂ removal require observational evidence beyond numerical runs and formally checked equations.

[C]Worthy · Open-source ocean science

Models, datasets, and reproducible workflows.

[C]Worthy develops open-source tools for investigating ocean CDR: where approaches might work, the risks they may carry, how much carbon they might remove, and how outcomes could be monitored and verified. The three codebases in this independent study address distinct parts of that work.

Convergent · RLT · AI · Formal verification

Making scientific software claims independently checkable.

Convergent Research provides a broader context for research programs that organize scientific infrastructure and accelerate scientific progress. Formalization and verification in science is also a research direction relevant to this project. The connection concerns shared scientific interests. This study remains independently organized, with future collaboration subject to each organization’s interest and approval.

Reading Level Test (RLT) records source-pinned claims, definitions, assumptions, expressions, and evidence boundaries. ChatGPT and Claude assist with code investigation, testing, counterexamples, review, and reporting. RLT's own kernel checks its certificates; Lean 4 checks selected more general mathematical propositions. Compiled reference programs, numerical comparisons, and GitHub CI test the actual software.
