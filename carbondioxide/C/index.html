Ocean carbon removal depends on physical measurements, mathematical models, and software that other people can examine. We are studying how to make the connections among those claims independently verifiable.

Independent research applying Reading Level Test (RLT) to [C]Worthy’s open-source C-Star, ROMS-Tools, and UCLA-ROMS projects. The original software is developed and maintained by [C]Worthy; this research is conducted independently.

The research

From scientific claims to checkable evidence.

[C]Worthy's software helps prepare, run, and analyze ocean simulations. RLT records exact claims, their assumptions and sources, and what independent checking actually establishes. We use each tool for what it can demonstrate.

01 · Model

C-Star + UCLA-ROMS

Model configurations, computational grids, simulation workflows, and reproducible inputs.

02 · Measure

ROMS-Tools

Ocean data preparation, carbon quantities, numerical conversions, and analysis of modeled uptake.

03 · Verify

RLT + Lean

Source-pinned reading points, kernel-checked arithmetic, selected general proofs, and documented correspondence scope.

Each form of evidence has its own role: mathematical proofs check specified relationships, numerical tests examine implementations, and model validation and observations establish physical correspondence.

Progress report · November 23, 2026

Independent audit and development.

We began with four source-pinned repositories, comparing scientific claims, mathematical relationships, and executable implementations. Findings, candidate corrections, and verification records are organized in local research branches for independent review.

  1. 001 · Assessment

    Source-pinned investigation Completed

    Mapped existing tests and provenance records, identified verification opportunities, and reported an initial mismatch between accepted grid configurations and input-file partitioning.

  2. 002 · Tiling

    Grid partitioning and joining Review

    Developed candidate repairs for uneven processor splits and reconstructing tiled files. The evidence package includes compiled-Fortran comparisons, regression tests, and RLT/Lean proof artifacts. The next stage is independent replay and integration review.

  3. 003 · CO₂

    Carbon units and conversion Repair

    Added an independent expected value for the tonnes-CO₂ conversion and documented its atomic-weight source. The current review is refining the supported unit dimensions and their regression tests.

  4. 004 · Geometry

    Vertical-coordinate correspondence Review

    Compared parameter conventions and numerical formulas with the UCLA-ROMS reference, including zero-parameter cases. Further work examines numerical stability for small parameter values.

  5. 005 · Uptake

    DIC-based carbon uptake Scientific review

    A candidate correction changes the reported uptake for releases that remove dissolved inorganic carbon. Scientific review is examining the intended uptake definition, conservation budget, and applicable assumptions.

  6. 006–007 · Evidence

    Provenance and independent verification In progress

    Tracing river-chemistry ratios and reviewing proposed repairs, test results, mathematical statements, and scientific meaning. Development is organized on dedicated research branches pending verification.

Evidence status: proposed patches are under independent review; reported tests are being replayed; modeled carbon uptake is interpreted alongside its assumptions and physical observations.
