Carbon dioxide · Ocean science · Verification

[C]Worthy

Ocean carbon removal depends on physical measurements, mathematical models, and software that other people can examine. We are studying how to make the connections among those claims independently verifiable.

Independent research applying Reading Level Test (RLT) to [C]Worthy’s open-source C-Star, ROMS-Tools, and UCLA-ROMS projects. The original software is developed and maintained by [C]Worthy; this research is conducted independently.

The research

From scientific claims to checkable evidence.

[C]Worthy's software helps prepare, run, and analyze ocean simulations. RLT records exact claims, their assumptions and sources, and what independent checking actually establishes. We use each tool for what it can demonstrate.

01 · Model

C-Star + UCLA-ROMS

Model configurations, computational grids, simulation workflows, and reproducible inputs.

02 · Measure

ROMS-Tools

Ocean data preparation, carbon quantities, numerical conversions, and analysis of modeled uptake.

03 · Verify

RLT + Lean

Source-pinned reading points, kernel-checked arithmetic, selected general proofs, and documented correspondence scope.

Each form of evidence has its own role: mathematical proofs check specified relationships, numerical tests examine implementations, and model validation and observations establish physical correspondence.

  1. 001 · Assessment

    Source-pinned investigation Completed

    Mapped existing verification, identified cross-repository correspondence questions, and established an initial grid-partitioning discrepancy.

  2. 002 · Tiling

    Grid partitioning and joining Accepted locally

    Reviewed uneven-grid partitioning, tile placement, and coarse-grid joining against UCLA-ROMS tools. Replays matched all 68 tested tilings across two grids.

  3. 003 · CO₂

    Carbon units and conversion Accepted locally

    Added an independently derived tonnes-CO₂ conversion test and an explicit dimensional units contract. Review also corrected case-sensitive prefixes, where Mmol and mmol differ by a factor of one billion.

  4. 004 · Geometry

    Vertical-coordinate calculations Accepted locally

    Added zero-parameter support and numerically stable stretching formulas in ROMS-Tools and UCLA-ROMS. High-precision checks and three full UCLA-ROMS analytical runs support the changes; upstream compiler and CI reference checks follow.

  5. 005 · Uptake

    DIC-based carbon uptake Scientific review

    An independent analytical model distinguishes additional air–sea CO₂ uptake from net DIC retained in a model domain. The proposed correction remains isolated while its intended scientific quantity and assumptions are clarified.

  6. 006–007 · Evidence

    Scientific provenance and verification Reviewed

    Traced source calculations, river-chemistry conventions, and numerical behavior; developed RLT certificates, Lean companion proofs, and independent replay evidence.

  7. 008 · Integration

    Independent acceptance Local candidates

    Replayed findings from fresh checkouts, repaired additional review findings, and assembled accepted patches into separate local ROMS-Tools, C-Star, and UCLA-ROMS integration branches. Scientific questions remain recorded for domain-expert review.

  8. 009 · Next

    Fork CI and contribution preparation Planned

    Run the integration branches through fork CI, inspect the remaining environment-dependent tests, and prepare individually reviewable upstream contributions.
