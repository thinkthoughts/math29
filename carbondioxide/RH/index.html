01 / Physical reading · mathematical claims

RH. What is proved. What is observed.

The Riemann Hypothesis concerns the zeros of a mathematical function. Living lungs, atmospheric CO₂, and geological carbon records are physical observations. RLT keeps their forms of evidence distinct.

RH: ζ(s) = 0 and 0 < Re(s) < 1 ⇒ Re(s) = ½
Verified external development · November 21, 2026

On November 21, 2026, the Lean and Mathlib communities jointly certified the statement and proof in Levent Alpöge’s Lean formalization of the Riemann Hypothesis. This page records that external verification, not a new RLT proof or an independent replay by this site.

01 / Context

A mathematical result does not become a physical measurement

The Riemann zeta function and its zeros are mathematical objects. The RH proof concerns their stated relationships. The carbon cycle, molecular composition, and human respiration are investigated through physical measurement and scientific models. Neither domain should borrow a verdict from the other.

02 / Reading points

Four distinct checks

Read the expression

RH asserts a property of all nontrivial zeros of ζ(s). Check the scope of “all,” the mathematical convention, and the formal statement.

Check the relationship

The Lean and Mathlib communities report independent proof checking and exact statement comparison for Alpöge’s formalization.

Read the physical record

CO₂ in exhaled breath, geological strata, and measured concentrations have observational evidence—not a theorem certificate for RH.

Respect human interpretation

Consent, sustainability, and observed outcomes involve people and physical systems. Proof validity does not establish those outcomes.

03 / Work ahead

RLT’s reading point

An exclusivity claim such as “all nontrivial zeros” has exact mathematical scope. It is not a license for universal claims about people or nature. Expand imaginations. Extend care. Resist illiteracy.
