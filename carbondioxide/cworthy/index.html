Earth · Physical context

Earth's carbon cycle, measured and modeled.

Earth's oceans exchange carbon dioxide with the atmosphere while circulating carbon through living organisms, seawater chemistry, sediments, and geological reservoirs. Ocean carbon dioxide removal (CDR) proposals seek to influence parts of that cycle. Whether an intervention produces durable additional removal—and what ecological consequences it may have—must be determined from physical evidence, appropriate observations, and adequately validated models.

Scientific software evaluates modeled interventions, quantifies uncertainty, and supports monitoring plans. Observational measurements establish physical outcomes; mathematical proofs and software tests establish properties of the models and implementations.

Convergent · RLT · AI · Formal verification

From scientific specifications to independently checked implementations.

Convergent Research advances focused scientific research organizations and infrastructure. Formalization and verification in science connects directly with the methods demonstrated here: source-pinned claims, checked mathematical relationships, adversarial testing, and reproducible computation. [C]Worthy's ocean modeling code provides the scientific application; RLT-CSTAR supplies independent verification evidence. Each organization retains its own research priorities and decisions.

Reading Level Test (RLT) records source-pinned claims, definitions, assumptions, expressions, and evidence boundaries. ChatGPT and Claude assist with code investigation, testing, counterexamples, review, and reporting. RLT's own kernel checks its certificates; Lean 4 checks selected more general mathematical propositions. Compiled reference programs, numerical comparisons, and GitHub CI test the actual software.
