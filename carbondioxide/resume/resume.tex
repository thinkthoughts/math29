\section*{Selected Mathematical Papers and Formal Results}
\entry{General Divisor Theorem (GDT)}{2026}
\subline{\site{https://goodmath.app/divisor.pdf}{divisor.pdf} \textbar{} \site{https://github.com/thinkthoughts/general-divisor-theorem}{Lean repository}}
Derives and formally checks results for admissible residue-conditioned sequences, including a minimal period, counts, density, and a totient correction identity:
\[
T_{\min}=mR,\qquad d=\operatorname{rad}(\gcd(m,N)),\qquad
R=\frac{\operatorname{rad}(N)}{d}.
\]
The Lean formalization passed an independent mechanical verification run, with documented correspondence between the formal and informal theorem statements.

\entry{translatePi --- Exact Rational Integration over Constrained Simplices}{2026}
\subline{\site{https://translatepi.ai/main.pdf}{translatePi paper} \textbar{} \site{https://github.com/thinkthoughts/lean-bgp212}{Lean source and verification records}}
Proves simplex-moment identities, rational representations of Gram entries, and selected exact certificates; develops symmetric-function and profile-based evaluation with independent Lean checks:
\[
\int_{\Delta_m(R)}\prod_{i=1}^{m}x_i^{e_i}\,dx
=\frac{R^{m+\sum_{i=1}^{m}e_i}\prod_{i=1}^{m}e_i!}
{\left(m+\sum_{i=1}^{m}e_i\right)!}.
\]
The paper documents certified components and identifies the full numerical Gram matrices and final variational inequality as subsequent research targets.
