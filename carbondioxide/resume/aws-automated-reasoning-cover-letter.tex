\sectionlabel{Two mathematical research foundations}
\textbf{General Divisor Theorem (GDT)}\\
\href{https://goodmath.app/divisor.pdf}{goodmath.app/divisor.pdf} \quad \textbar\quad local: \texttt{resume/divisor.pdf}
\[
T_{\min}=mR,\qquad d=\operatorname{rad}(\gcd(m,N)),\qquad
R=\frac{\operatorname{rad}(N)}{d}.
\]
The theorem specifies a minimal period for the admissible residue-conditioned sequence under the paper's hypotheses. Its Lean formalization includes period, count, density, and correction-factor results.

\textbf{translatePi --- Exact Rational Integration over Constrained Simplices}\\
\href{https://translatepi.ai/main.pdf}{translatepi.ai/main.pdf} \quad \textbar\quad local: \texttt{resume/translatePi.pdf}
\[
\int_{\Delta_m(R)} \prod_{i=1}^{m}x_i^{e_i}\,dx
=\frac{R^{m+\sum_{i=1}^{m}e_i}\prod_{i=1}^{m}e_i!}{\left(m+\sum_{i=1}^{m}e_i\right)!}.
\]
This is Theorem 2.2 (\texttt{simplex\_integral}) of the uploaded paper, for $m\in\mathbb N$, $R\geq 0$, and $e\in\mathbb N^m$. The paper also documents formal results on rational Gram entries, symmetric polynomial bases, and certified coefficient calculations, while identifying remaining work.

\sectionlabel{From formalized mathematics to an independent prover}
I designed RLT to distinguish \emph{written expressions}, \emph{reading conventions}, \emph{proved relationships}, and \emph{physical evidence records}. Its proof-checking kernel and explicit assumptions make it possible to inspect what was proved, under which rules, and where source correspondence or physical applicability is established.

Additional engineering includes a Lean toolchain port and strict certification workflow for \textbf{39/39 theorems} in DeepSeek-Prover-V2, with independent kernel replay, and Lean models and automated tests for AI inference behavior in DeepInfer.

\sectionlabel{Where the research extends automated reasoning}
I can advance automated reasoning where proof validity, specification fidelity, and evidence provenance meet. The formalized physical reading point \href{https://carbondioxide.now/RLT/}{``CO$_2$ is exhaled''} illustrates how RLT separates a written empirical claim from checked arithmetic and the evidence necessary to support its physical correspondence. The aim is to extend the engineering statements that verification systems have provided—without overstating what a proof certifies.
